I need collection and delivery

Free collection and delivery for diesel injectors, pumps and other components, so you don’t have to bring them to the workshop.

Ask about collection: 023 8061 9655 Request a free estimate

How it works

Daily collection and delivery

A daily delivery and collection service runs when you need it, from our industrial premises in Eastleigh, close to the M3 and M27 motorways.

Express courier (UK only)

If you’re outside the daily delivery area, we can offer an express courier service anywhere in the UK. Request a collection and we’ll email you a collection label. Collection is free for parcels up to 30kg.

Online collection request: coming soon. For now, call 023 8061 9655 to arrange a collection.

Is my location covered?

Call the workshop and tell us where you are. We’ll let you know whether you’re on the daily route or whether the courier service is the better option.

What to tell us

  • Your company name (if any), contact name and phone number
  • The collection address, including the postcode
  • The part, such as an injector, pump or other component, and the part number if known
  • A short description of the fault
