About us

Diesel fuel injection specialists

Supplying, reconditioning and testing all makes of diesel fuel injection equipment since 1974.

M&B was appointed Bosch Diesel Service Agents in 1976 and holds Bosch Diesel Centre status, quality audited every year. It became a Delphi Diesel Centre in 2008 and has since been appointed a Centre of Excellence. The Eastleigh premises, close to the M3 and M27, include a modern workshop, test shop, garage and clean room.

Free collection and delivery

Free estimates

Request a free estimate

M&B gives free estimates for repairs to fuel injection equipment on car, commercial and marine units, and for auto electrical units. From a minor fault to a major failure, the team can answer your questions or offer advice.

It helps to have these ready:

  • Your vehicle make, model and registration, or the machine the part came from
  • The symptoms, such as a warning light, lack of power, smoke, bad starting or a fuel leak
  • The part number from the injector or pump, if you know it
  • Whether you need the part collected, or want it removed and refitted
