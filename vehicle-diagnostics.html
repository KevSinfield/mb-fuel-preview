My vehicle has a diesel fault

M&B’s diagnostic equipment checks that your engine’s electronic control systems are working, and uses stored fault codes to find the problem on most vehicles on the road today.

Call 023 8061 9655 Request a free estimate

Faults we diagnose

Call the workshop if you’re seeing any of these:

  • Engine management light on
  • Lack of power
  • Poor fuel consumption
  • Bad starting
  • Excessive smoke
  • Erratic idle
  • Fuel leaks
  • Seized or blowing injectors
  • Low fuel pressure

The workshop uses Bosch KTS670, Delphi DS350 and Denso DSTPC diagnostic equipment.

Removal & fitting

Fuel pumps, injectors and related parts can be removed, serviced and refitted to your vehicle, either at our premises or on site. Smoke emission testing to MOT standards is also available.

More about removal & fitting

Seized injector removal

Using the latest equipment, we can remove injectors that are seized in the cylinder head, snapped or damaged. The work can be done at your premises or in our workshops. Genuine replacement injectors are available, plus a re-coding service.

More about seized injector removal

DPF cleaning

Cleaning your existing diesel particulate filter recovers up to 98% of its original efficiency, which can save the cost of a replacement. The service covers cars, heavy goods vehicles, plant and agricultural machinery.

More about DPF cleaning
